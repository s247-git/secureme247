## Common Computer Issues Reston Businesses Face

Understanding the most common computer problems helps you prepare and prevent them. Here are the issues we see most frequently with Reston businesses.

### Hardware Failures and Repairs

Hardware failures are inevitable. Hard drives fail, power supplies burn out, screens crack, and keyboards stop working. The question is not whether hardware will fail, but when and how quickly you can get back up and running.

Common hardware issues include:

- Hard drive and SSD failures causing data loss
- RAM failures causing system crashes and blue screens
- Power supply failures preventing systems from booting
- Battery degradation in laptops reducing mobility
- Monitor and display failures
- Peripheral failures (keyboards, mice, printers)

Professional computer support includes rapid diagnosis and replacement. Many providers keep spare components in stock so repairs happen in hours, not days.

### Software and Operating System Issues

Software problems are the most common cause of IT tickets. They range from simple application crashes to complex operating system corruption.

**Operating system issues** include slow boot times, update failures, driver conflicts, and blue screen errors. These often stem from outdated drivers, corrupted system files, or incompatible software installations.

**Application issues** include Microsoft Office crashes, accounting software errors, line-of-business application failures, and browser problems. Many of these can be resolved remotely without interrupting the user workflow.

**Malware and virus infections** remain a persistent problem. Even with antivirus software, new threats emerge daily. Professional computer support includes active threat monitoring and rapid cleanup when infections occur.

### Network and Connectivity Problems

When a computer cannot connect to the network, it is effectively useless. Common network-related issues include:

- Wi-Fi connectivity drops or slow performance
- VPN connection failures for remote workers
- Printer and shared drive access problems
- Email configuration and delivery issues
- DNS resolution problems causing slow browsing

Many connectivity problems are caused by misconfigured network settings, outdated drivers, or interference. A good computer support technician can diagnose and resolve these issues quickly.

## Preventative Computer Maintenance Programs

The best way to avoid computer problems is to prevent them before they happen. Preventative maintenance programs keep your systems healthy and reduce emergency repairs.

A comprehensive maintenance program includes:

- **Scheduled updates** Operating system and application updates applied on a regular schedule with testing to prevent compatibility issues
- **Disk health monitoring** Tracking SSD and hard drive health metrics to predict failures before they happen
- **System cleaning** Removing temporary files, clearing caches, and optimizing storage
- **Hardware inspection** Checking for dust buildup, fan failures, and other physical issues
- **Performance optimization** Tuning startup programs, memory usage, and system settings
- **Security scans** Regular malware and vulnerability scanning

Reston businesses on a preventative maintenance plan experience 60-80% fewer emergency computer issues compared to those relying on break-fix support alone. The investment in prevention pays for itself many times over in reduced downtime.

## Computer Support vs. IT Support: Understanding the Difference

The terms computer support and IT support are often used interchangeably, but they have different scopes.

**Computer support** focuses on the computers themselves workstations, laptops, peripherals, and the software running on them. Computer support technicians handle hardware repairs, software troubleshooting, operating system issues, and basic network connectivity.

**IT support** is broader. It includes computer support plus network infrastructure, servers, cloud services, cybersecurity, compliance, and strategic technology planning. IT support covers the entire technology ecosystem of your business.

Most Reston businesses need both. You need computer support for day-to-day workstation issues and IT support for your overall technology strategy. ## The Cost of Computer Support in Reston

Computer support costs vary based on the number of devices, the level of service, and whether you need on-site support.

**Per-incident repairs** typically cost $100-$200 per visit for diagnosis and repair. This is the most expensive option in the long run because you pay for every problem individually and there is no preventative maintenance.

**Per-device plans** cost $30-$75 per computer per month. These include monitoring, maintenance, and help desk support. Repairs may be included or billed separately for parts.

**Managed IT plans** cost $150-$250 per user per month and include computer support plus full IT management, network monitoring, cybersecurity, and help desk. This is the most cost-effective option for businesses with more than 5 employees.

For Reston small businesses, a managed IT plan is typically the best value. You get predictable monthly pricing, proactive maintenance that prevents problems, and rapid response when issues do occur.
