## What Are Managed IT Services?

Managed IT services mean outsourcing your company’s technology management to a specialized provider called a Managed Service Provider (MSP). Instead of hiring, training, and retaining an in-house IT team, you pay a predictable monthly fee for a comprehensive suite of IT services.

Think of it as having an entire IT department for a fraction of the cost.

### What is Typically Included

**Help Desk & User Support**
Your employees get access to a professional help desk for troubleshooting. Password resets, software issues, connectivity problems, new user setup. All handled quickly by technicians who know your environment.

**24/7 Network Monitoring**
Your network, servers, and endpoints are monitored around the clock. Problems are detected and addressed before they cause downtime.

**Cybersecurity**
Modern managed IT includes endpoint protection, email security, firewall management, and often advanced services like Managed Detection and Response (MDR). Security is no longer an optional add-on. It is the foundation of any good MSP agreement.

**Backup & Disaster Recovery**
Automated backups, immutable storage, and tested recovery procedures ensure your business survives any data loss event: ransomware, hardware failure, or natural disaster.

**Patch Management**
All your systems receive timely security updates. Automatic where possible, manual where needed. No more chasing critical patches.

**Strategic IT Planning**
Quarterly business reviews, technology roadmaps, and budget planning. Your MSP becomes a strategic partner, not just a break-fix vendor.

## How to Choose a Managed IT Provider in Reston

### 1\. Verify Local Presence

The provider should have a physical office in Reston or immediate proximity. Visit their office. Meet the team. If they cannot come to your location within 4 hours, keep looking.

### 2\. Check Industry Experience

Ask about experience with your industry. A defense contractor needs CMMC expertise. A dental practice needs HIPAA knowledge. A SaaS company needs SOC 2 readiness. Choose a provider with relevant experience.

### 3\. Evaluate Security Maturity

Ask about their own security posture. Do they use MFA internally? Do they have a SOC? Do they run background checks on employees? A provider that cannot secure itself cannot secure you.

### 4\. Review the Service Level Agreement (SLA)

The SLA defines what you are buying. Look for:

- Defined response times for each severity level
- On-site dispatch commitments
- Scheduled maintenance windows
- Reporting requirements
- Termination terms (no long-term lock-in)

### 5\. Ask About Staffing

Who will support your account? Do you get named technicians who know your environment, or do you call a 1-800 number and hope for the best? Named, US-based support is worth paying for.

### 6\. Get a Proof of Concept

Most quality MSPs offer a free assessment or pilot period. Take them up on it. See how they respond. Evaluate their tools and reporting. The proof is in the performance, not the sales pitch.

## Cost of Managed IT Services in Reston

Pricing varies widely based on the scope of services, number of users, and compliance requirements. Here is a rough guide for Reston businesses in 2026:

### Small Business (10-25 Users)

- **Essential:** $250-$500/month (endpoint protection, email security, basic help desk)
- **Professional:** $500-$1,000/month (adds 24/7 monitoring, MDR, backup)
- **Premium:** $1,000-$2,000/month (adds compliance management, vCISO, advanced security)

### Mid-Size Business (25-100 Users)

- **Professional:** $1,000-$2,500/month
- **Premium:** $2,500-$5,000/month
- **Enterprise:** $5,000+/month (custom deployments, dedicated resources)

### What Affects Pricing

- Number of users and devices
- Industry and compliance requirements
- Existing technology and any remediation needed
- On-site support frequency
- Cloud vs. on-premises infrastructure

Most quality MSPs offer a free assessment to scope the right solution before quoting a price. Avoid providers that quote blind without understanding your environment.
